# IJGIS article-type decision

Official text (Instructions, 2026-11-10): Foresight papers "bring new perspectives and stimulations to GIScience research ... caution research pitfalls ... may address the following in a reflective or thought-provoking way: the context ... the hook ... the opportunities". They receive expedited review.

## A. Research Article
- Fit: the manuscript is a full empirical study (Methods with 12 subsections, 9 figures, 5 tables, clustered inference, two null families). Its claims rest on quantitative stress tests, not on argument.
- Risk: an editor may read it as methodological rather than a new algorithm. Mitigation: the Introduction/Discussion now state the GIScience problem (validity of optimization on constructed geographic information products) and the bounded novelty claim.

## B. Foresight Paper
- Fit: the central message cautions a research pitfall (optimization gain read as proxy validity).
- Against: the official description frames Foresight papers as reflective/thought-provoking pieces organized around context-hook-opportunities. It does not state that a full empirical study is an expected Foresight format. Recasting would require compressing the empirical architecture that carries the evidence, which conflicts with "Retain quantitative evidence" and the frozen-science rule.

## Decision rule applied
The prompt says choose Foresight only if current guidance *clearly permits* empirical evidence to support a methodological warning. The guidance is silent on empirical content and describes a reflective format, so the condition is not clearly met.

**Final article type: RESEARCH ARTICLE.** Used consistently in the title page, cover letter and handoff. The choice is based on format fit, not on perceived acceptance odds; the Foresight-style research directions are kept as one Discussion subsection.
