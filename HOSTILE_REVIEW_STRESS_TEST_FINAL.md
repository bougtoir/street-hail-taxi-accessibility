# HOSTILE REVIEW — STRESS-TEST FINAL

1. "Merely reporting that maxima exceed starting values?" MANAGEABLE — that
   fact is precisely the object of study; the paper quantifies how much of
   the apparent gain is structural vs transferable vs non-random.
2. "Mathematically obvious?" MAJOR — partially true; the contribution is
   empirical demonstration and calibration (how close to the null the real
   surface lies), not the theorem. Stated honestly in 4.1/4.3.
3. "What does the permutation test add?" RESOLVED — it separates prevalence
   (marginally non-random) from magnitude (fully generic); without it the
   large gains would be misread as evidence.
4. "Why is cross-specification transfer meaningful?" MANAGEABLE — distinct
   public-data component families are alternative plausible readings of
   'opportunity'; near-chance off-diagonal transfer exposes specification
   dependence that ablation alone hides.
5. "Why should CEUS readers care about taxis?" MANAGEABLE — the taxi case
   instantiates a general proxy-optimization problem; the framework, not the
   taxi, is the contribution (Discussion 4.5/4.6).
6. "Does the framework generalize?" MANAGEABLE — tests require only a
   constructed surface + candidate sets; directly applicable to
   accessibility, safety, EV, micromobility proxies.
7. "Is the paper too negative?" MANAGEABLE — the finding is constructive:
   it supplies concrete checks and shows the proxy retains modest
   reliability above null plus real information beyond road hierarchy.
8. "Without ground truth can anything be concluded?" MANAGEABLE — the
   paper's claim is deliberately internal: it characterizes what
   self-optimization can and cannot establish. No stronger claim is made.
9. "Are ROAD and ATTRACTOR really distinct?" MANAGEABLE — rank agreement
   only 0.52-0.73 (distinct constructions); the residual shared dependence
   on mapped urban form is acknowledged.
10. "Over-generalizes from one city?" MANAGEABLE — findings are reported
    for one city; the framework is the transferable element and city
    specificity is a stated limitation.
