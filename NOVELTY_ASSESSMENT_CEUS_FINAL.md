# NOVELTY ASSESSMENT — CEUS FINAL

Contribution is the combination, claimed explicitly:
- telemetry-free public-data construction of a street-hail opportunity score
  on a directed road-segment representation;
- passenger-side pedestrian relocation formulation;
- a validation architecture (component ablation, cross-specification
  transfer, spatial permutation null) separating structured urban signal
  from optimization tautology.

Not novel and not claimed: pedestrian relocation itself (T-Finder and later
systems recommend relocation), accessibility indices, kernel attractor
fields.

Positioning vs closest work: T-Finder (TKDE 2013) requires taxi GPS
trajectories; this framework requires none and is designed for settings
where such data cannot be obtained. The audit apparatus (transfer +
permutation null) is the methodological differentiator most relevant to
CEUS's geospatial-computation readership.

Honest scope: the demonstrated signal is moderate — reliability marginally
above a spatial null, weak off-diagonal transfer. Novelty is architectural,
not empirical.
