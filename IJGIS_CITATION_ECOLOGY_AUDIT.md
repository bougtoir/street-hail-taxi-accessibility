## Gaps relative to IJGIS framing

1. Foundational GIScience framing of geographic information as a constructed representation: only a textbook (Longley et al. 2015). The new Introduction claims these surfaces are "geographic information products in the GIScience sense" — this needs a primary GIScience source.
2. Propagation of input/specification uncertainty through GIS models to outputs: no GIS error-propagation source, although the argument ("an optimum inherits the construction") depends on it.
3. Reproducibility/replicability of geospatial research: the Reproducibility section and the Discussion of representation choice cite only psychology/statistics sources (Simmons, Steegen, Simonsohn, Gelman and Loken), not geospatial ones.
4. No IJGIS paper in the bibliography.
