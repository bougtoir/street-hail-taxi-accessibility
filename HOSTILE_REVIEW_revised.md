# HOSTILE PEER REVIEW — REVISED

1. **"The model optimizes a score it created itself."**
   Major → manageable. Explicit: we optimize a constructed opportunity score
   and show the *contrast structure* is robust across four substantially
   different constructions. The score is a hypothesis object, not a measured
   quantity. Central claim rephrased to "candidate spatial contrasts".

2. **"The taxi-flow prior is not actually taxi flow."**
   Resolved. Renamed to "public-data-derived circulation-potential proxy";
   every instance audited (manuscript, captions, appendix, docs). No
   observed-flow language remains.

3. **"The result is driven entirely by road hierarchy."**
   Manageable. ROAD-ONLY ρ 0.73 vs combined; attractor-only construction —
   which ignores hierarchy — still yields 99% improved origins and beats
   nearest-major 99% of the time at 300 m. Signal is not reducible to
   hierarchy.

4. **"The result is driven entirely by POI density."**
   Manageable, symmetric: ROAD-ONLY (no attractors) still yields 95% improved
   origins at 300 m. Neither family alone explains the result.

5. **"Walking to the nearest major road explains everything."**
   Manageable. Combined optimizer exceeds nearest-major in 60–89% of origins
   (Wilcoxon p≈0, bootstrap CI excludes 0). Honest bound retained: the optimum
   is itself major-class in 76% of origins at 500 m — stated plainly.

6. **"The accessibility units have no behavioral meaning."**
   Acknowledged, not fatal. Units are z-scores; primary metrics changed to
   absolute SD-unit gain, fraction improved, and distance-to-threshold. All
   percentage-gain headlines removed; RAG demoted to secondary where |A0| is
   interpretable, with a methods note on denominator instability.

7. **"Temporal heterogeneity is input-driven."**
   Resolved by reframing: temporal patterns are now presented as modeled/
   assumed-profile consequences ("under the modeled weekend specification"),
   moved from primary results to descriptive model behavior.

8. **"The study does not validate actual taxi waiting."**
   Acknowledged by design; Discussion gains an explicit "what this study does
   not establish" subsection listing vacant-taxi passage rates, waiting-time
   reduction, hail success probability, behavioral compliance.

9. **"The paper duplicates earlier taxi relocation systems."**
   Manageable. T-Finder et al. need fleet telemetry; nothing in the cited
   literature builds a telemetry-free opportunity ranking with an
   ablation-based robustness audit. Positioning narrowed accordingly.

10. **"Why publish this before field validation?"**
    Defensible: the ablation shows candidate contrasts are robust — a
    precondition worth publishing before committing to fieldwork. The paper
    explicitly positions itself as computational groundwork; Paper 2 is the
    validation study.

## Residual fatal risks: none identified.
Residual major: the circulation proxy is still unobserved — irreducible in a
dry study; flagged as the first thing Paper 2 must measure.
