# SCIENTIFIC CONTRIBUTION — EVIDENCE CHECK

CLAIM: Self-optimization produces large apparent gains.
→ SUPPORT: COMB 300 m: 98.4% origins improved, mean +1.50 SD, clustered
  95% CI [1.46,1.54]. Similar across radii/specs.
→ LIMITATION: structural order-statistics effect; ~98% of prevalence
  reproduced by permutation null.
→ APPROVED.

CLAIM: Cross-specification transfer is weak/asymmetric.
→ SUPPORT: ROAD→ATTR +0.04 SD (53% positive); ATTR→ROAD +0.08 SD
  (44%) — near-chance between component families; proxy transfers
  stronger because the proxy embeds road hierarchy.
→ LIMITATION: applies at 300 m between distinct families.
→ APPROVED.

CLAIM: Spatial nulls reproduce much of the apparent benefit.
→ SUPPORT: 500-permutation weekday_17: frac improved obs 0.983 vs null
  0.979 at 300 m (p=0.074; 0.030 at 200 m, 0.086 at 500 m, 1.0 at 100 m);
  null mean gain 2.61 SD exceeds observed 1.50 SD at all radii.
  Within-ward null: same pattern.
→ LIMITATION: prevalence advantage is small and radius-dependent;
  interpretation not dichotomized at p<0.05.
→ APPROVED.

CLAIM: Nearest-major-road does not fully account for the combined score.
→ SUPPORT: optimum beats nearest-major for 81.5% of origins at 300 m,
  +0.22 SD, CI [0.21,0.23], Wilcoxon p<0.001.
→ LIMITATION: internal structure only; no implication for real taxi
  encounters (stated in text).
→ APPROVED.

CLAIM: Internal stress tests (ablation, transfer, spatial null, heuristic
  benchmark) are a practical auditing framework.
→ SUPPORT: each test changed the interpretation (transfer revealed
  specification dependence; null revealed generic heterogeneity).
→ LIMITATION: presented as internal checks, not sufficient validation.
→ APPROVED.
